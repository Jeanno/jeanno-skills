# Realign

A correction means your underlying assumption was wrong. Comply, diagnose, and align before resuming:

1. **Comply immediately**: Stop, undo, or halt exactly as asked. No defending or justifying past choices.
2. **Isolate broken assumption**: State in one sentence the exact assumption that was disproved.
3. **Grill the misalignment**: Run `/align` scoped strictly to that assumption and its dependent decisions.
4. **Sweep for siblings**: Check other completed or planned tasks resting on the same faulty assumption.
5. **Confirm before resuming**: Restate the corrected understanding and get explicit user approval before touching code.
